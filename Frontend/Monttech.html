OUR TECHNOLOGY

Intelligent Monitoring for Modern Agriculture

The IOT19 Smart Environment System monitors important environmental and agricultural parameters using connected sensors and intelligent technologies. The collected information can be used to understand field conditions, improve resource management and support agricultural analysis.

MONITORING MODULES

Seven Technologies Working Together

Each module focuses on an important part of the agricultural environment.

01 • IRRIGATION

Soil Moisture Monitoring

Soil moisture sensors monitor the amount of moisture available in the soil. The information can support automatic irrigation according to crop requirements.

  • Soil moisture measurement
  • Irrigation monitoring
  • Water conservation
02 • WATER MANAGEMENT

Water Level Monitoring

The water level module monitors water available in storage tanks and helps maintain proper water availability.

  • Tank level monitoring
  • Water availability
  • Irrigation support
03 • ENVIRONMENT

Light Intensity Monitoring

Light intensity measurements help analyze the lighting conditions available in the agricultural environment.

  • Light measurement
  • Environmental analysis
  • Crop growth support
04 • CROP ANALYSIS

Crop Growth Monitoring

Image-based observation can be used to monitor crop development and field conditions.

  • Crop image analysis
  • Growth observation
  • Field monitoring
05 • CLIMATE

Temperature & Humidity

Temperature and humidity monitoring provides information about environmental conditions affecting plant growth.

  • Temperature monitoring
  • Humidity monitoring
  • Environmental analysis
06 • AI DETECTION

Pest Detection (YOLO11s • best (1).pt)

Deep learning object detection leveraging Ultralytics YOLO11s trained weights (best (1).pt) to classify 102 distinct species of agricultural insect pests, identify infestation hotspots, and estimate damage severity.

  • Model Checkpoint: best (1).pt (YOLO11s)
  • 102 Class Agricultural Taxonomy
  • Real-time Inference (< 300ms) with FastAPI Microservice
  • 896×896 Resolution with Bounding Box Localization
🐛 Test best (1).pt (YOLO11s) 🌿 Test plant_disease_model.pt (ResNet-18)
🌿 Plant Disease Diagnosis Integration:

Paired with plant_disease_model.pt (PyTorch ResNet-18 architecture) trained across 38 PlantVillage disease classes to evaluate foliage health and deliver automated treatment remedies.

07 • WEB PLATFORM

IoT Control & Web Platform

The web platform brings monitoring information together in one centralized interface.

  • Centralized monitoring
  • IoT connectivity
  • Dashboard interface
